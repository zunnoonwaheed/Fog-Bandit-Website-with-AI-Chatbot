PROTECTION WHEN YOU NEED IT

Protect Your Store, Whether You're Open or Closed.

Robberies and break-ins don't only happen after hours. Fog Bandit can provide an additional layer of protection whether your team is working or the store is closed.

While Your Store Is Open

If someone attempts a robbery during business hours, a properly configured Fog Bandit system can be activated to quickly reduce visibility and make valuable stock harder to locate.

After You've Closed

If someone breaks in after hours, Fog Bandit can activate through a compatible alarm system, filling the protected area with fog and making it harder to see what's inside.

Fog Bandit works alongside compatible alarms and other security measures to add another layer of protection.

No. Simply submit your details and our team will contact you within 1–2 business days.

Can Fog Bandit work with my existing alarm system?

Fog Bandit can work with compatible alarm and security systems. Our team can review your current setup and explain what's possible.

How is Fog Bandit activated?

Depending on your setup, Fog Bandit can be connected to compatible intrusion or panic-alarm systems. Our team can explain the available options for your store.

We welcome enquiries from businesses across Australia. Our team will confirm installation and service availability for your location.
